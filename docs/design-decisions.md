## Interface architecture

### USB

USB4105 D+ pins join and D- pins join at the receptacle, then pass through a
USBLC6-2SC6 low-capacitance ESD array to CP2102N. CC1 and CC2 each receive their
own 5.1 k pull-down to ground. SBU pins are NC. VBUS passes connector-side TVS/
ESD protection and TPS2553 current-limited switch. CP2102N is externally powered
from 3.3 V with VBUS sensing wired exactly per the chosen package's datasheet.
UART TX/RX go to ESP32 UART0. DTR/RTS drive the proven two-NPN Espressif network
whose truth table prevents both asserted modem signals from permanently holding
EN low. BOOT and EN buttons independently pull GPIO0 and EN low. Provide named
test points on UART TX/RX, EN, GPIO0, 3V3, protected 5V, and GND.

### CAN_HS

GPIO16/17 connect directly to TCAN332 TXD/RXD. Place 100 nF at VCC. Route through
two 0 ohm series links in Rev A, then a PESD2CAN24T-Q at the connector. These
links reserve a routing break but are not a drop-in four-pad choke footprint. A clearly
labeled shunt/header enables one 120 ohm resistor across CANH/CANL; default is
open. Connector is CANH, CANL, GND. Verify choke/TVS capacitance against the
maximum intended CAN rate during Phase 2.

### CAN_FT

ESP32 VSPI connects only to MCP2515; active-low INT uses GPIO13 with an external
pull-up. MCP2515 TXCAN/RXCAN connect to TJA1055T/3 TXD/open-drain RXD, with the
required 3.3 V pull-up on RXD. TJA EN and STB are on GPIO33/32 with defaults to a
non-driving low-power state; ERR goes to GPIO35 with a 3.3 V pull-up. Unused WAKE
ties to BAT as NXP recommends; INH is left open. VCC and BAT use +5V_FT.

Fault-tolerant termination is **not** 120 ohm across the pair. Provide matched
1% local resistors from CANH to RTH and CANL to RTL. NXP requires the parallel
sum over the entire network to be about 100 ohm per line and states each local
resistance should be 500 ohm to approximately 6 kohm. Rev A captures matched
1.2 kohm resistors behind two normally-open solder jumpers; bridge both only
after the intended network topology is known. This is the ten-node-network
candidate, not a universal termination value.
Add matched line capacitors/protection only if validated against AH0801.

### Display

DFRobot DFR0486 connects through a keyed PH2.0-4P Gravity cable: +3V3, GND,
SCL(GPIO22), SDA(GPIO21). The module is intended for enclosure/front-panel
mounting rather than being cantilevered from the controller PCB. Its published
schematic shows 10 kΩ I2C pull-ups behind the onboard level shifting, so board
4.7 kΩ pull-ups R24/R25 are DNP. Firmware uses SSD1306 at address 0x3C.

## Major risks

1. Video timing and DAC amplitude under a 75 ohm load, especially with radio
   coexistence and the undocumented JVC format acceptance.
2. ESP32-WROOM-32E-N4 stock/certification continuity and correct 32E footprint
   implementation (lower risk than the original NRND module).
3. USB bus-power compliance during coincident ESP32 radio and CAN activity.
4. TJA1055's unusual dual 5 V/BAT and distributed termination requirements.
5. DFR0486 availability, cable retention, and enclosure/front-panel fit.
6. Ground-loop noise between USB ground and an automotive head unit chassis.
